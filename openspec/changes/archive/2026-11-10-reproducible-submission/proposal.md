## Why

The final evaluation includes a reproducibility review and 30% qualitative criteria (pipeline description,
code quality); entries that cannot be reproduced do not pass (DESIGN.md section 5, public notebook "CHAMPION_PAYLOAD"
entry). The submission must be a CSV that the scorer accepts and a notebook must regenerate it from raw
images and stored weights. Landed 2026-11-04 (notebook byte-identical to submission 4); kept current through
submission 8 (2026-11-10).

Source: DESIGN.md section 3 item 8, section 5 entries "Notebook reproducibility (2026-11-04)", "Submission
5/7/8 candidate".

## What Changes

- `filseg.predict` writes `submissions/<name>.csv` with columns `filament_id,segmentation_rle` (COCO RLE of a
  2048 x 2048 mask), validates it (unique ids, known test stems, non-empty masks) and records runtime per image.
- Refiner weights stored as float16 to fit the repository (49-52 MB each).
- `pipeline.ipynb` (generated by `scripts/make_notebook.py`) runs inference from the raw test JPEGs and
  `weights/`, and writes a CSV that matches the submission.

## Non-goals

- Training inside the notebook (training commands are listed, not run); GPU-only features.

## Falsified by

The notebook CSV differing from the submitted CSV, or the validator accepting an empty, duplicate or
unknown-stem row.

## Cost

Notebook run on CPU: 8.1 s per image for submission 4 while a training job shared the machine; submission 8
runs 15.9 s per image on MPS on a loaded machine.
